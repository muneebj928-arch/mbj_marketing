Shipping and returns

Delivery

Major cities: 3 to 5 working days. Other areas: 5 to 8 working days. You will get a message on WhatsApp when your parcel is dispatched.

Payment

Cash on delivery. You pay the rider when the parcel arrives.

Returns and exchanges

If an item arrives damaged or does not fit, message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. Keep the item unused and in its packaging, and we will arrange a return or exchange.

Contact

WhatsApp or email us at the details on the checkout page and footer. We reply within one working day.
